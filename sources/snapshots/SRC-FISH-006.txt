Kerala Fishermen's Welfare Fund Board,(KFWFB)
Kerala Fishermen’s Welfare Fund Board (KFWFB) works under Fund Act, 1985 providing for the constitution of welfare of Fishermen in the State.
Fishermen's welfare schemes
Fishermen's Welfare Fund Board (Fisheries Board) are carried out in accordance with the provisions and rules of the Kerala Fishermen's Welfare Fund Act , 1980 , the Kerala Fishermen's Welfare Fund Act , 1985, the Kerala Fishermen's Welfare Fund Scheme, 1986 and the Fishermen's Amendment Act, 1999.
